# Observe whether the improvement helps

This is a proposed private evaluation, not completed research or public submission copy. No participant, feedback, time saving or result has been invented.

## Who and what

Invite someone who actually reviews invoices or incoming payments for a freelance or small-business operation. Use the fictional sample, not their confidential records. Ask permission to take notes; do not contact anyone or share records automatically.

Give them the task without showing the answer: identify which invoices PAY-001 should cover using the supplied information, inspect the effect on other payments, then undo the decision. Let them choose whether to use the guide. Avoid coaching while observing.

## Record actual evidence

- Can they explain why two groups total 200 and why that does not establish the correct group?
- Can they find the remittance and distinguish its references from verified payer identity?
- Can they predict what the chosen group does to PAY-005 before confirming?
- Do they understand that the app reserves invoices here without posting to accounting?
- Can they inspect the changed payment and undo without help?
- Which term, label or control causes a pause or wrong action? Record assistance, mistakes and task completion accurately.
- Ask what their current workflow already does better, and whether this review is useful in that workflow. A positive comment is not proof of adoption.

Record date, relevant experience, actual observations and changes made. Publish quotes, identity or customer information only with permission. Compare task time only if a comparable baseline is actually measured; do not infer time saved from a fast fictional demo. A single observation can reveal a usability issue but does not establish broad demand.
