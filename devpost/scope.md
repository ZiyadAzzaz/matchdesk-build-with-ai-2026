# MatchDesk (working label)
A local payment-review desk for a freelancer or small business working from invoice and bank CSV exports.

## Authorization
Participant explicitly approved the researched payment-workspace direction and requested implementation and two prior-project backups. These details are agent proposals under that delegation. Draft means the document has not been personally reviewed; do not fabricate approval or interviews. User authorization overrides repeated Skill Pack interview pauses, not its authorship/integrity boundaries.

## The Unique Kernel
Make ambiguous allocations visible: a 200 payment might cover a 200 invoice or invoices of 75 + 125. Show exact alternatives and the original rows, let a person add remittance evidence and confirm a proposed allocation, then prevent those invoices from being used twice. This is not a new matching category; existing tools overlap. The design advantage must be demonstrated.

## Who It's For
A person manually reviewing a modest batch of positive outstanding invoices and incoming payments in one or more supported two-decimal currencies. They already have exports; no institution or bank integration is needed.

## The Core Loop
Load fictional example or preview/import two CSVs -> select payment and customer scope -> inspect candidate invoice groups -> add evidence/choose a group -> acknowledge and confirm locally -> review remaining exceptions -> export decisions or undo a decision.

## Inspiration & Identity
Participant wants clickable, friendly, visibly polished controls. Agent direction: dark ink sidebar, warm white workspace, teal primary actions, amber uncertainty, compact payment list, invoice allocation cards and persistent review inspector. System fonts; no external assets or decorative fake charts.

## What "Working" Looks Like
An actual ambiguity computed from input rows is resolved after reading a clearly fictional remittance, choosing the named invoices and explicitly confirming. A second payment cannot reuse them. Export reflects the decision, input hashes and undo history. A unique sum still requires human review.

## The POC Boundary
CSV only, explicit import mappings, bounded subset search, exact cents, one currency per match, exact selected customer scope, local session memory, proposed-allocation exports. No account, subscription, bank connection, ledger write or paid API.

## Explicitly Cut
PDF/OCR and fuzzy payer guessing: extraction/identity uncertainty. Fees, partials, credits and FX: accounting semantics not established. Automatic posting/payment collection: unauthorized and unnecessary. AI certainty scores: no evidence supporting calibrated confidence. Novelty, time saved, customer adoption and prize predictions: unproven.
